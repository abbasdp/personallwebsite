Install party to set up [#DeltaChat](<https://techhub.social/tags/DeltaChat>) servers, created several local test servers, and promoted it.

This was our community's final in-person gathering of the year for free software enthusiasts, held privately. We designated the year 1404 (in the Persian calendar) as the year for promoting free software culture.

![Two people at a private free software community gathering, testing and registering DeltaChat servers on their mobile phones](/static/media/posts/20/install-party-phones.jpg)

Based on this, we organized various events and conferences to introduce this culture, and similarly pursued diverse promotional approaches to amplify the voice of free software.

The adoption of free software has had positive impacts in Iran. Now, with a record of 500 active Delta Chat servers during Iran's internet blackout period, we made an effort to hold this final gathering.

![A laptop on a table, displaying a command line interface for installing a DeltaChat server.](/static/media/posts/20/install-party-terminal.jpg)

We have named the new year as the year for promoting decentralized [#free_software](<https://techhub.social/tags/free_software>) tools and will continue to pursue it.

May the new year in a free Iran allow us to both host events introducing books like [#Ada](<https://techhub.social/tags/Ada>) and [#Zangemann](<https://techhub.social/tags/Zangemann>), and promote free, decentralized tools.
